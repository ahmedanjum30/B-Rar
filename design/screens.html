B-Rar · three directions

Each one is buildable with Windows' own tools: solid colours, rounded shapes, Segoe UI, the Fluent icon font, Windows' list and progress bar. The logo stays. Pick a direction (or a screen within one) and I'll build every screen in it.

A · Windows 11

Looks like Explorer and Settings: a command bar, the list as a white layer with rounded top corners, Windows' accent blue, filled Fluent status icons, buttons in a darker footer strip like Windows 11 dialogs. The closest to "part of Windows".

B · Workbench

For people who live in archives, in WinRAR's tradition: a toolbar of big icon buttons, an address bar, a dense list with column dividers and alternate rows, a status bar with fields. The progress window shows two bars (total and current file) and the numbers in a grid.

C · Navy band

The logo's navy becomes a band across the top of every window: the toolbar and breadcrumb in the browser, a large percentage over an edge-to-edge bar in the progress window. Underneath, a calm blue-grey surface. The most "B-Rar", the least like stock Windows.
